# SwipeClean Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 20 November 2026
**Contact:** mindlogicsolutions@gmail.com

This policy explains how the SwipeClean app ("SwipeClean", "the app") handles information. SwipeClean is a photo and video organiser for Android and iOS.

## Summary

- **Your photos and videos never leave your phone.** SwipeClean has no server, no cloud storage and no analytics.
- **Signing in with Apple or Google is optional.** Your name and email stay on your phone; SwipeClean does not send them anywhere.
- The free edition shows ads from **Google AdMob**. Google's advertising software processes some device and usage information to show and measure ads.
- **SwipeClean Pro** is an optional one-time purchase that removes all ads. Payment is handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store.

## Information SwipeClean accesses on your device

With your permission, SwipeClean reads your photos and videos so you can sort them. For each item it uses its system identifier, whether it is a photo or video, the date it was taken, its duration (videos) and its file name. It also reads album names and counts.

SwipeClean does **not** read locations, file contents beyond what is needed to show the item on screen, faces or other metadata. If you grant access to selected photos only, it sees only those.

## Information stored on your device

Everything SwipeClean stores is kept **only on your phone**, in the app's own storage:

- your review decisions (Keep, Delete folder, Important, Later) and when you made them;
- your settings;
- the number of items you have deleted with SwipeClean;
- for the free edition, three numbers used to space out ads;
- whether this device has a verified Pro purchase;
- if you signed in: the provider (Apple or Google), its user ID for you, your name and your email, kept in the phone's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore).

Uninstalling the app removes all of it. SwipeClean cannot see or restore it afterwards.

## Information SwipeClean sends

**SwipeClean itself does not send any of your information anywhere.** Your photos, videos, file names, dates and decisions are never transmitted, including to the advertising and payment providers below.

### Optional sign-in: Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In

Signing in is optional and only shows your name in the app; it unlocks nothing and syncs nothing. The sign-in happens between you and Apple or Google, under their own privacy policies ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy)). SwipeClean receives your name, email address and a user ID from the provider, keeps them only on your phone and never sends them to any server. With Sign in with Apple you can hide your email address.

**Sign out** or **Remove sign-in from this phone** (Settings → Account) deletes them from your phone. Remove also revokes SwipeClean's access to your Google Account. For Apple, you can stop using your Apple Account with SwipeClean in iOS Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple.

### Advertising (free edition): Google AdMob

The free edition uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform (for consent). After you have made 20 swipe decisions, SwipeClean starts the ad software; where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), Google's consent form is shown first.

According to Google, its SDK automatically collects and shares, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

- your device's **IP address** (which may be used to estimate general location);
- **app interactions** such as app launches, taps and ad views;
- **diagnostic information** about app and SDK performance;
- **device identifiers** such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

This information is encrypted in transit. Google processes it under its own policies: [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

SwipeClean requests **non-personalised ads**. Where consent applies, you can change your choices at any time in **Settings → SwipeClean Pro → Ad privacy choices**. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the system settings. On iOS, SwipeClean does not ask for tracking permission.

SwipeClean Pro users are not shown ads, and SwipeClean does not start the ad software for them. (On Android, a small start-up component of the ad software is run by the system when the app launches.)

### Purchases: Google Play and the App Store

If you buy SwipeClean Pro, payment is handled entirely by Google Play Billing or the Apple App Store. SwipeClean never receives your card details, name or email address. The app only asks the store whether your account owns Pro, and stores the result on your device.

## Deleting photos and videos

Swiping never deletes anything. Items are deleted only when you choose to delete them from the Delete folder, confirm in SwipeClean, and confirm again in the system's own dialog. On Android, items deleted with SwipeClean are removed **permanently** and do not go to a Recycle bin; on iOS they go to Recently Deleted. SwipeClean cannot restore deleted items.

## Children

SwipeClean is not directed at children under 13.

## Data retention and deletion

SwipeClean does not keep any of your information on a server, so there is nothing for us to delete. Information on your device is removed when you uninstall the app. For information processed by Google for advertising, see Google's policies linked above.
